What protects your money

A missed payment is not a loss. Two things stand in the way, and both are set for each project on its own merits.

The first is a deliberate gap
We never lend the full value of what is pledged. An independent valuer prices the asset, and the loan is set below that figure. How far below depends on how the asset would sell — a building holds its price, a set of looms does not, so it is discounted harder. The gap is what absorbs a bad sale.

The second is the clock
From the first day a payment is missed, penalty interest accrues on everything outstanding. It is paid to you out of the proceeds before any principal is returned. Delay is charged for — it is not absorbed by you.

How a loan gets onto this platform

Two origins, one structure.

Some loans we write ourselves. Others were written by a Romanian IFN months earlier and the claim was assigned to us, then to you. Either way you hold the claim against the company, and either way we collect it.

What the rule will not do

Worth reading before you leave it watching for a year.

01It does not judge a borrower. A rate above your floor is not a sign of quality — often it is the opposite. The rule matches numbers, and numbers are all it can see.
02It will offer you anything that qualifies. Including a company you would refuse on reading the page. The list arrives pre-ticked, so untick before you approve rather than after.
03A pledge is a commitment. Once a repayment is pledged to an open opportunity you cannot redirect it. You can cancel the pledge any time before the money arrives — after that it is invested.
04It gives you no priority. Commitments are filled in the order they were made, whether they came from an approved list or from someone reading the list themselves. Being notified first does not put you at the front.

Listing rules · both ways

A loan in trouble cannot be sold to anyone.

May be listed
Every payment made on its due date — not within a grace period, on the date.
Nothing outstanding: no arrears, no accrued penalty interest, no fee unpaid.
The collateral registered and the charge in force, re-checked by us.
No enforcement, insolvency or restructuring open against the borrower.
Delisted the same day
One day late is late. A listing is withdrawn automatically the morning a payment is missed, mid-sale included.
The seller keeps the loan and keeps the penalty interest. Nobody is handed a problem they did not price.
It can be listed again only after the arrears and the penalty interest are fully paid.
We do not permit a discount to stand in for a disclosure.
This costs you the option of selling your way out of a bad loan — deliberately. A market where distress can be repriced and passed on is one where reading the listing carefully is the only thing protecting you, and most people will not.

What it means here

Diversification

Splitting your money across several loans instead of putting it into one. It does not make any single loan safer — the collateral behind a loan is the same whether you put € 100 or € 2,600 into it. What it changes is how much of your money is stuck waiting if one borrower stops paying.

The interest is the same in all 3 rows. Only the last column changes — and the last column is 8 to 14 months of a court process with your money inside it.

Diversifying across 26 loans that all lend against the same kind of asset in the same city is less useful than it looks. Your portfolio is currently 51.1% real estate, 41.0% vehicles and equipment, 7.9% with no asset pledged at all — that spread matters as much as the count.
