Every loan here is secured against something real and priced below an independent valuation, but a forced sale can raise less than expected, a court process takes 8 to 14 months, and some loans have no asset pledged at all — only the owner's promise. What reduces the risk is the collateral margin and the penalty interest, not any guarantee from us. There is no deposit protection here.
Penalty interest of 0.10% to 0.20% a day starts accruing on whatever is outstanding, and it is collected out of the proceeds before your principal. We contact the company, and if it cannot catch up we enforce the charge over the collateral and sell it. Through the Romanian courts that has taken 8 to 14 months in the 5 cases we have run, returning 54% to 71% of the valuation.
Not from the borrower — the schedule is the contract. You can offer your position to another investor on the secondary market, provided the loan is paying to the day: a loan with any arrears or accrued penalty interest cannot be listed, and a listing is withdrawn automatically the morning a payment is missed. So the exit exists while a loan is healthy and closes exactly when you would most want it. We charge 1.89% of what you receive. There is no guaranteed buyer and no guaranteed price. Of the 84 listings in the last 90 days, 61 sold.
