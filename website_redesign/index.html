What lending here means

Plain terms, before you give us an email address.

You choose the company
From € 100 per loan. Nothing is ranked, scored or recommended — we are paid by the borrower, so any recommendation would be one we profit from.
Money arrives monthly
Interest and your own capital back, together, on the loan's own schedule. Every figure shown on the platform is contracted, not forecast.
If it stops paying
Penalty interest of 0.10% to 0.20% a day accrues in your favour, and the pledged asset — valued independently, lent against at 45 – 65% — is enforced. You can lose money; there is no deposit protection.
